Effective date: 2026-09-21  ·  Last updated: 2026-09-21

The short version. We store your account details, your conversations, and your in-app progress so the app works. To generate replies we send your conversation text to a third-party AI model provider. We do not collect your IP address, your device identifiers, or your location. We do not sell your data.

2. What we collect

2.1 Information you give us

CategoryWhat exactlyWhy
Account Nickname, avatar image, the name you use for yourself in roleplay, and your self-description. To identify you and let characters address you correctly.

  • Your in-app currency balance and its transaction history.
  • Your affinity level with each character, and which side stories you have unlocked.
  • Quest, achievement and notification state.
  • Ad-reward and free-message quota counters (these reset daily).

2.3 What we do not collect

We do not collect or store:
  • your IP address;
  • device identifiers (advertising ID, IMEI, Android ID and similar);
  • your location;
  • your contacts, photos, microphone or camera (unless you yourself upload an image);
  • your browsing history outside the App.

4. How long we keep it

  • Conversations and memories — until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Account data — until you delete your account, after which it is removed from our active systems.
  • Purchase records — retained as long as required for tax and accounting obligations, even after account deletion.

5. Your choices and rights

You can, at any time:

  • edit or delete individual conversations and memories from within the App;
  • delete content you have posted;
  • request a copy of your data, or request deletion of your account and its data, by writing to eddie@lurvy.top.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have additional rights under the GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what we collect and the right to request deletion. We do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address above; we will respond within the period required by applicable law.

Our legal bases for processing are: performance of a contract (running the App you asked for), legitimate interests (keeping the service secure and working), and consent where required — for example, for personalised advertising in regions where consent is legally required.

6. Advertising and consent

The App shows rewarded advertisements, which you choose to watch in exchange for in-app currency. Where required by law — in particular in the EEA and the UK — we ask for your consent before personalised advertising is enabled, and you may withdraw that consent at any time. Where consent is not required, you can still limit ad personalisation through your device settings.

7. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 17. It contains mature themes and romantic and suggestive content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 17. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
