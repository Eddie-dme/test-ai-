Delete your account

Applies to Vesperine (top.lurvy.vesperine)  ·  Operated by Lurvy Limited

In short. You can delete your Vesperine account and all associated data at any time. There are two ways to do it: from inside the app, or by email if you no longer have the app installed. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes the following from our servers:

DataWhat it is
Account recordYour email address, nickname, roleplay persona, password hash
ConversationsEvery message you sent and every reply you received, plus the chat sessions they belong to
MemoriesFacts, summaries and diary entries generated from your conversations
Content you createdCharacters, scenarios, feed posts, comments, likes and saves
AlbumImages generated for you, and their prompts
ProgressHearts balance and ledger, affinity levels, unlocked chapters, quest and achievement state
Social dataFollow relationships, blocks, and read markers
Safety recordsReports you filed, and moderation records associated with your account
SessionsAll login tokens, so any device still signed in is signed out
Retention exception. Records we are legally required to keep — for example tax, accounting or law-enforcement obligations — may be retained for the period the law requires. Moderation records relating to child safety may be kept longer where the law obliges us to preserve them. Everything else is deleted immediately.

How to delete your account

Option 1 — In the app fastest

  1. Open Vesperine and sign in.
  2. Open the side menu (the ☰ button, top left).
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the side menu.
  4. Tap Delete account (shown in red).
  5. Confirm. Deletion happens immediately.

You will be signed out on every device as soon as deletion completes.

Option 2 — By email if you no longer have the app

Send an email to eddie@lurvy.top with the subject line:

Account deletion request

Send it from the email address registered to your account, so we can verify the request belongs to you. If you cannot send from that address, tell us the account email in the message and we will ask you a verification question instead.

We process email requests within 7 days and reply to confirm once the data is gone.

What we cannot delete on request

Two things are outside a single account's control, because they do not belong to you alone:

  • Content other people copied. If another user saved or quoted something you posted publicly, that copy is part of their account.
  • Aggregate counts. Like counts and view counts are stored as numbers without any link back to who contributed them.
